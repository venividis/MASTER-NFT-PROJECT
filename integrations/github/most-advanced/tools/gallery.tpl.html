IPSEITY

A four-dimensional solid, an instrument for turning it, and one contract holding both. Every plate below came back out of a contract call — the stills were drawn by Solidity, and the instrument on the stage is the exact document a marketplace receives when it asks for tokenURI().

The instrument

What the holder opens

Press start and the page inflates itself in your browser, reads the state block the renderer wrote into it, and draws. Drag to turn the solid; the slider under the field moves the cutting plane through w.

The stills

Eight solids, eight plates

One token per solid. Each square is the SVG the contract returns as the token's image — no raster, no server, drawn from the same 128-bit word the instrument reads. Pick one and the stage and the page follow it.

What you are looking at

  
All of it is on chain, and none of it is on a chain right now

  

    

      
Real

      
The stills, the elevations, the traits, the state blocks and the document on the
        stage were all produced by running the contracts on an EVM at Cancun and reading
        the results back. Nothing on this page was drawn by hand.

    

    

      
Not real yet

      
There is no deployed collection behind this frame. The instrument will find no
        wallet and no endpoint, so every control that would write to a chain reports that
        it has nowhere to write to. Everything that only looks, works.

    

    

      
Sealed and open

      
A token is born with six of its twelve instruments unsealed. Half the plates here
        have had all twelve opened, half are as minted — the difference is visible in the
        rail down the left of the stage.
